About the company

A well-established accounting firm in Cape Town is seeking a detail-oriented Tax Administrator to join its team.

The role

This is a great opportunity for someone with a strong understanding of South African tax compliance to grow within a professional accounting environment.

Key responsibilities

  • Preparing and submitting tax returns for individuals and companies
  • Managing SARS correspondence and eFiling
  • Maintaining accurate client tax records
  • Assisting with provisional tax and VAT submissions
  • Liaising with clients and SARS to resolve queries

Requirements

  • A relevant qualification in tax, accounting or finance
  • At least two years' experience in a similar role
  • Strong knowledge of SARS eFiling and tax legislation
  • Excellent attention to detail and organisational skills
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and accounting software

What's on offer

  • Salary of R18,000 – R23,000 per month, depending on experience
